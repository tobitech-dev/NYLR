Vessels Unto Honour

NYLR is the National Young Leaders Retreat of the Redeemed Christian Church of God, brought to you by DTCE Global. It gathers young leaders for three days, from 6th to 8th November, 2026, at Redemption City, and it is held on a country and regional basis.

This year's theme, Vessels Unto Honour, comes from 2 Timothy 2:20-21. In a great house there are vessels of gold, silver, wood, and earth, and the passage shows that a person who stays clean and set apart becomes a vessel fit for the Master's use. The retreat is about becoming that kind of leader: pure, prepared, and ready for every good work.

If a man therefore purge himself from these, he shall be a vessel unto honour, sanctified, and meet for the master's use, and prepared unto every good work.2 Timothy 2:20-21
